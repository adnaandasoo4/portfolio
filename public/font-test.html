PP Neue Machina is a commercial font from Pangram Pangram with no free CDN. To preview it here:

  1. Go to pangrampangram.com/products/neue-machina and download the free trial pack.
  2. Save PPNeueMachina-PlainUltrabold.woff2 to public/fonts/test/neue-machina/.
  3. Uncomment the @font-face block at the top of this file.
  4. Reload — this card will render in Neue Machina.
Trial license covers personal / development preview only. Production use needs a paid license.
